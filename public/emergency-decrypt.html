🔐 Lockt Emergency Viewer

Read-only access to a Lockt backup, without the app, a sign-in, or an internet connection. Everything happens in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded or saved.

This browser can't run the decryption (Web Crypto is unavailable). Open this file in a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

A lockt-backup-… file (.json or .txt) from Settings → Backup & Restore, or lockt-data.encrypted downloaded from OneDrive (Apps → Lockt).

Your data is visible on screen. Close this tab or press Close & clear when you're done. It clears itself after 10 minutes without activity.
